What is a Sequence and a Term?

A sequence is a list of numbers arranged in a specific pattern order.

Each individual number in a sequence is called a term.

Lesson 5 of 6

Mastering the Algebraic Rule (dn + c)

To find any rule of the form $\text{Term} = \mathbf{d \cdot n + c}$:

  1. Find the common difference ($d$): How much the sequence increases each step.
  2. Test with $n = 1$: Multiply $d \times 1$.
  3. Adjust ($c$): Add or subtract to match the very 1st term!
